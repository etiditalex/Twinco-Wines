# Catalog sources

The wines in `src/content/catalog.ts` are a labelled preview, not TwinCO’s stock list. Descriptions and tasting notes on the site are original. The fields below were checked against the public pages named here in November 2026. Vintage labels move. Where a fact sheet and a retailer disagree, both are named and the bottle label should win.

Preview prices and stock counts were invented for testing. They are not TwinCO selling prices and they are not taken from these sources.

Directory listings were used only to locate the Nyali shop. They are not a product source. AfricaBiz lists TwinCO Wines on Links Road with +254 720 044444 and a set of hours. GoAfrica and a DHL service-point page give different hours. Those contacts are not published as verified. See `src/content/store.ts`.
